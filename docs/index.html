Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. Party Baby runs entirely on your device — no connection required, before or during a game.

Does the app save my progress?

Your best night (stars, grade, and feeding record) is saved locally on your device so you can track your progress across sessions. There's no account and nothing is ever sent anywhere — see the privacy policy for details.

I found a bug, or have an idea for the game

Send it over — screenshots or a quick description of what happened are both genuinely useful. Feature ideas are welcome too.
