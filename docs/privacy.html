🍼 Party Baby / Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026

The short version

Party Baby doesn't collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Your best-night stats are saved only in local storage on your own device — nothing is ever sent anywhere.

What Party Baby is

Party Baby is a single-player game about getting through a long night with a newborn. It runs entirely on your phone. There is no login and no internet connection required to play.

Information we collect

None. Party Baby does not collect any information about you or your device. Specifically, the app does not:

  • Require or support creating an account
  • Use analytics, crash reporting, or tracking tools of any kind
  • Make network requests of any kind while you play
  • Access your location, contacts, camera, or microphone
  • Collect advertising or device identifiers

What's stored on your device

Party Baby saves your best night's results (star rating, sleep grade, and feeding record) using your device's local storage, purely so the app can show you your personal best. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is removed if you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

Party Baby does not currently use any third-party SDKs, ad networks, or analytics services. If that changes in a future update — for example, if advertising is added — this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what's collected and why, and the effective date above will change accordingly.

Children's privacy

Party Baby does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will appear on this page with a new effective date. Meaningful changes — like the addition of advertising or analytics — will also be reflected in the app's next update notes.
