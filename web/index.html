How to Play

The goal

Get from 8:00 PM to 8:00 AM. Feed the baby exactly 4 times, and keep the baby asleep as much as possible.

Each turn

The baby signals a need. Pick the response that matches it. Every option falls somewhere on a scale from best down to worst — the better the read, the more sleep you bank. Miss badly, and the baby parties instead of sleeping.

Feeding

Offering a bottle always counts as a feed, whether or not the baby was actually hungry — so feeding at the wrong moment still uses up one of your 4, and costs sleep besides.

Scoring

Your Sleep Grade (letter grade) reflects how much of the night the baby actually slept. Your star rating is stricter — it only hits 10/10 if every single decision all night was the single best choice available.
